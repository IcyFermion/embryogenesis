\caption{\textbf{Additional reconstruction strategies and null comparisons.}
Solid curves reproduce the two Figure 8 methods; dashed curves show three
additional cached strategies. Each curve contains its own sampled
non-dominated cost pairs; no cross-method union is presented as a common
feasible-set optimum. Layerwise assignment and the degree-constrained forest
use 301 weights; the additional cached methods use 1,001 weights. Top-down
rebuilding nearly overlaps the forest. Bottom-up-by-layer assignment permits
internal identities, including root identities, to move on a fixed topology.
Paired bottom-up rebuilding does not explicitly fix biological root identities;
its final coverage and component scope require a separate audit. These are
algorithmic comparisons with differing constraints, not equally constrained
alternatives certified by the publication pipeline. All five null/reference
families are shown: first-cousin shuffle, internal-layer shuffle, full
assignment shuffle, random rebuild and the adopted separate-clock Gaussian
reference. Dots are deterministic display subsets; plus signs use the full
available cost-distribution means. Coordinates use first-cousin-shuffle
standard deviations and place the natural lineage at the origin, with
continuous axes. The full-assignment and random-rebuild clouds nearly
coincide. The six-method inventory table records constraints and audit status.
The terminal-only method is listed there but omitted from this plot: it
constructs internal spatial midpoints, does not fix measured root states,
and its current implementation can omit the last scored pair. Its cached
optimized curve has not been regenerated or certified. Supplementary numbering
is provisional until manuscript integration.}
\label{fig:ce_full_tree_heuristics_supplement}
